Lip & Facial Accents

4 treatments

Signature Lip Blush

A semi-permanent watercolor tint that adds soft, youthful flush and defined symmetry ("your-lips-but-better"). Lasts 1–3 years with 20-min pre-numbing; custom-blended to your unique undertone.

At your consultation, Preethi tests shades on your lips to achieve your ideal nude-pink or soft rose tint. If you have darker or uneven pigmentation, we pair this with lip neutralization so the color heals true.

Enquire about Signature Lip Blush ↗

Dark Lip Neutralization

Targeted corrective pigments to cancel cool, dark, or patchy hyperpigmentation caused by sun exposure or melanin variations, restoring an even, warm base tone.

Preethi evaluates your natural lip melanin. Neutralization ensures that subsequent lip blush tints heal clean and radiant rather than muddy or dull.

Enquire about Dark Lip Neutralization ↗

Bespoke Beauty Spot

A delicate, semi-permanent cosmetic beauty mark (mole tattoo) placed to accentuate facial symmetry, eyes, lips, or cheekbones with classic elegance and zero daily makeup.

Preethi pencil-maps prospective placements with you in the studio mirror first. We custom-blend pigment to match your natural melanin undertone so the mark heals looking authentically yours.

Consultation &Preparation Guide.
