THE CONSULTATION GUIDE

Choosing your treatment

How do the brow techniques differ?

Microblading focuses on individual hair-like strokes. Combination brows pair strokes with shading. Ombré brows use graduated shading for a more filled-in look.

What should I mention if I already have permanent makeup?

Tell Preethi which treatment you had and when. Ask whether a touch-up or another approach is appropriate for the existing work.

Can I enquire if I haven’t chosen a treatment?

Yes. Select “Help me choose” in the enquiry form and tell the studio what you would like to change.
