CLIENT NOTES

Before your studio visit

01

Bring your everyday look

A photo of how you usually fill your brows or the lip colour you reach for most can make it easier to describe what you want.

02

Share your treatment history

Mention any previous permanent makeup or recent skincare treatments. Ask whether anything should change before your appointment.

03

Ask about the days after

Before choosing a date, ask the studio about the aftercare instructions and when to check in about healing or a touch-up.

AFTER YOU ENQUIRE

From enquiry to appointment

  1. Share your preferences

    Choose a treatment or describe the brow, lip or skincare result you’re interested in.

  2. Send your enquiry

    Submit your details online or message the studio on WhatsApp.

  3. Confirm with the studio

    Ask the studio about availability, preparation and the exact address before making your visit.
