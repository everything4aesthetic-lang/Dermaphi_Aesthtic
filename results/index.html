WHAT TO EXPECT

The Healing Transformation

Permanent makeup heals in stages. Understanding the natural skin cycle helps you appreciate every phase of your new look.

01 · Immediate Finish

Fresh pigment appears 20–30% darker and crisper right after your appointment with slight rosy warmth.

02 · Gentle Healing

Around days 4–7, light micro-flaking occurs as the skin heals naturally. Color softens into a soft, sheer tone.

03 · Healed Radiance

By week 4, the true stabilized pigment emerges. A complimentary touch-up perfects any fine hair strokes or color depth.
