THE PORTFOLIO GUIDE

How to read a before-and-after photo

Look at the shape

Compare how the brow starts, arches and tapers. Save an example that matches how you like to wear your brows.

Check the photo stage

Ask whether the image was taken immediately after the appointment or after healing. Use that context when discussing the finish.

Bring a reference

Tell Preethi what caught your eye in the photo. It could be the softer front, the definition through the tail or the lip colour.

What would you like to ask Preethi?

“I like the shape in this photo. Can we keep the front of my brows softer?” Bring a question like this, or send us a reference you like.
