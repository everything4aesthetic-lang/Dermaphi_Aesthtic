A NOTE FROM OUR FOUNDER

A vision rooted in artistry and natural beauty

At Dermaphi Aesthetics, my vision is to celebrate individuality through the art of enhancing natural beauty. As a certified PhiBrows Artist, Permanent Makeup Specialist and Trainer, I believe every face has its own character. Every enhancement should be thoughtfully designed to complement it.

I bring precision, creativity and a commitment to continuous learning to each treatment. I’m equally passionate about sharing what I know and helping aspiring artists develop their skills through professional PMU training.

Thank you for being part of the Dermaphi journey. I look forward to hearing what feels right for you.

Preethi Sadha · Founder and PMU Artist

What does your everyday look like?

Some clients want to fill a small gap in their brows. Others want the soft definition they usually create with a brow pencil. Preethi starts with what you like about your features, what you’d like to change, and how much definition you want to see day to day.

THE DERMAPHI APPROACH

How to prepare for your consultation

  1. Bring a reference

    A photo can help you explain the shape, colour or finish you prefer.

  2. Mention previous permanent makeup

    Tell Preethi what you had done and when, so you can discuss the next step together.

  3. Bring your questions

    Ask about the treatment, preparation, aftercare and whether a follow-up may be needed.
