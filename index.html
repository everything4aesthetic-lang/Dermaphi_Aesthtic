CHROMEPET, CHENNAI

Permanent makeup in Chromepet

At Dermaphi Aesthetics, you can talk through brow shape, lip colour or scalp definition with Preethi Sadha, a certified PhiBrows artist. The studio also offers customised facials and skin treatments.

Microblading or ombré brows?

Microblading gives you individual hair-like strokes. Ombré shading gives a soft powder finish. Combination brows blend the two.

Compare eyebrow treatments ↗
Lip blush or lip neutralization?

Lip blush adds a tint. Lip neutralization focuses on balancing the appearance of uneven lip tone. Ask Preethi how your starting colour affects the options.

Before you book

What should I bring?

Bring a photo of the brow shape or lip shade you like. Tell us about any previous permanent makeup or recent skincare treatments.

What can I ask about?

Ask which finish suits your goals, what the session involves, and what to plan for aftercare.

How do I request a time?

Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp. Share the treatment you’re considering and your preferred day. The studio will confirm availability.
